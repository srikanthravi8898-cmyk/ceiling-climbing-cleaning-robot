# Ceiling-Climbing Cleaning Robot 🤖

## Project Overview
A ceiling-climbing cleaning robot designed to clean ceiling surfaces using a motor-driven suction mechanism and a controlled robotic platform.

## Objectives
- Develop a reliable suction mechanism for ceiling adhesion.
- Integrate BLDC motor and ESC-based speed control.
- Design a lightweight mechanical structure.
- Develop embedded control for safe and controlled movement.
- Evaluate adhesion, stability, power consumption, and cleaning performance.

## Hardware
- RS2205 2300KV BLDC motor (proposed for suction generation)
- Electronic Speed Controller (ESC)
- Battery and power distribution system
- Propeller or custom impeller (under evaluation)
- Robotic chassis and cleaning mechanism
- Microcontroller and sensors (to be finalized)

## Development Status
**Status:** Ongoing project — design and prototyping.

Components, control architecture, and performance specifications will be updated as development progresses.

## Testing and Validation
Planned tests include suction performance, ceiling adhesion, motor speed control, battery endurance, and cleaning effectiveness.

## Future Improvements
- Closed-loop motor speed control
- Improved adhesion and fail-safe mechanisms
- Automated movement and obstacle detection
- Runtime and energy-efficiency optimization

## Safety
Initial testing will be performed on a secured test surface with a safety tether and appropriate propeller protection.
